About this drill

Unofficial. This is an independent study aid. It is not made, checked or endorsed by the Civil Aviation Authority of Singapore (CAAS) or any training organisation, and its questions are not the real test questions.

Rules change. Facts were checked against public sources on : the Air Navigation Act 1966, the Air Navigation (101 – Unmanned Aircraft Operations) Regulations 2019, CAAS advisory circulars, ICAO weather-code standards and FAA study guides. Several drone rules changed in 2025. Before you fly or sit the test, confirm anything that matters on caas.gov.sg.

Your data stays on this device. Progress is saved in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the drill works offline once loaded.
